# Information Map: fictional worked example

From reader Chapter 26. Separate business concepts, meanings, stewardship and evidence from physical fields. These are proposed or illustrative teaching records, not actual agreements or outcomes.

| Business concept | Meaning needed for this decision | Proposed steward or deciding role |
|---|---|---|
| Requirement | The condition to be met under the applicable approved rule | Policy owns rule meaning and version changes |
| Submission | Evidence offered for review against a requirement | Admissions operations defines what can be received and reviewed; privacy/access conditions still apply |
| Receipt | A statement that the submission was received under a defined intake contract | The intake/service owner defines the receipt fact; it cannot approve satisfaction |
| Review decision | An authorized judgment about the submission against the requirement | Authorized review role records the decision; Engineering cannot infer it from an upload |
| Unresolved question | A question the current explanation or evidence cannot settle within the staff member's role | Sending staff retains responsibility until the receiving arrangement defines the next owner |
