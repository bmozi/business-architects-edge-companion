# Outcome Charter: fictional worked example

From reader Chapter 1. Define the person, outcome, evidence, unknowns and next decision. These are proposed or illustrative Harbor records, not actual agreements or outcomes.

| Charter field | Harbor's initial agreement |
|---|---|
| People affected | Admitted learners preparing to start, plus employees supporting them |
| Desired outcome | Learners can complete the requirements needed to start, with fewer unresolved obstacles |
| Observed problem | Staff report conflicting instructions, repeated checks, and unclear exception ownership |
| Current baseline | Not yet established; the reports identify investigation needs, not their frequency or causes |
| Initial investigation | Review an authorized sample of cases and trace where guidance or exception handling breaks down |
| Measurement responsibility | The onboarding business owner arranges an authorized analyst to define the cohort and collection method |
| Target | Agree after reviewing the baseline and constraints; do not invent a percentage |
| Guardrails | Protect personal information, retain accessible help, and avoid unowned support work |
| Decision ownership | Onboarding sponsor owns the proposed business change; policy and technical owners retain their respective authority |
| Next decision | Choose a bounded intervention or gather more evidence if the cases do not support a choice |
