# Explanation Card: fictional worked example

From reader Chapter 2. Connect audience and message to source meaning, authority and a changed-case check. These are proposed or illustrative teaching records, not actual agreements or outcomes.

| Question | Harbor's proposed answer |
|---|---|
| Who needs the explanation? | A learner who wants to know the next action needed to prepare to start |
| What must it help them do? | Identify a verified next step or reach an authorized person when the situation is unresolved |
| Which facts support it? | The relevant requirement, its approved source, and appropriately current learner-specific status if the tool is authorized to access it |
| What does the explanation not decide? | It does not establish eligibility, waive a rule, change a record, or authorize a start |
| Who owns the meaning? | The designated policy/business owners validate the distinctions; engineering implements the agreed contract |
| What if the facts are missing or disagree? | State the limitation and route the learner through the approved support path |
| How will we check understanding? | Ask a reviewer to explain what the message permits and what action follows, then test an unfamiliar exception |
