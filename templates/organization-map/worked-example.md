# Organization Map: fictional worked example

From reader Chapter 29. Connect units and roles to capabilities while verifying actual commitments. These are proposed or illustrative teaching records, not actual agreements or outcomes.

| Functional grouping | Role to identify and verify | Capability contribution | Commitment this map does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Policy function | Owner authorized to approve R7 meaning | Maintain approved requirement meaning | Approval of a specific new message, review timing or permission to expose it |
| Learner support | Staff/channel owner and receiving or escalation role | Explain current status and next action; route unresolved cases to help | Capacity for new case types, a new channel or continuous coverage |
| Admissions operations | Authorized evidence reviewer and receiving owner | Review submitted evidence; receive an agreed subset of unresolved cases | Acceptance of every question or a guaranteed decision time |
| Engineering and operating functions | Technical change owner and runtime/stop owner | Support the implementation of explanation and routing | Authority to reinterpret policy or accept another unit's workload |
| Security/privacy function | Role approving protected-use conditions | Support access and information boundaries across the capabilities | Permission for every staff member or model to see every submission |
