# Career Evidence — Chapter 32

Connect a claim to permitted evidence, contribution and observed/proposed/simulated status.

1. Read the chapter and inspect the [fictional worked example](worked-example.md).
2. Copy [worksheet.csv](worksheet.csv). The retained first-column prompts guide your work; replace case-specific terms when needed.
3. Fill the empty cells from permitted evidence. Label assumptions and unknowns, and name who can validate each consequential statement.
4. Ask a receiving stakeholder or skeptical colleague what would change the decision. Record the revision in your dossier.
5. Use the [chapter's worked answer](../../answers/chapter-32.md) after attempting its exercise.

A completed worksheet does not create approval, receiving capacity or a measured result. AI may draft from authorized inputs; people validate meaning and accept commitments. Keep a manual route usable.
