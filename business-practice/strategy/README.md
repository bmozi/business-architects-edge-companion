# Chapter 27: Connect strategy to execution

This is a self-study kit for the book's reader-facing Chapter 27. All Harbor facts and counts are invented teaching material. No private workplace data, paid account or AI tool is required.

## Work the paper case first

A fictional help page is deployed. Nine of twelve reviewed messages use approved current wording. Four of six sampled unresolved cases reach a named receiver with agreed context. There is no earlier sample, comprehension study or start outcome. Calculate both fractions; distinguish deliverable, operating indicator, handoff indicator and stakeholder outcome. Draw the five-link trace and mark at least two assumptions.

1. Read the chapter, then spend about 30 minutes on your own answer.
2. Copy [worksheet.csv](worksheet.csv), fill the fields and retain your assumptions. Use a spreadsheet or plain text; blank template cells are for your work.
3. Challenge your first answer: If the project is complete but the outcome is flat, who still has authority to act? Can the measure miss a person who never reached the queue?
4. Revise before opening the [worked answer and rubric](worked-answer.md). Compare reasoning and evidence boundaries rather than copying a preferred conclusion.
5. Repeat with a safe fictional or sanitized case of your own. More than one conclusion can earn full credit when the reasoning is justified.

## Deliverable

One strategy trace, separately defined measures and denominators, delivery/outcome owners, a simpler next step and an evidence gate.

AI may propose or challenge from permitted inputs. People validate meaning, owners, funding and commitments. An invented source, automatic approval or missing receiver does not become evidence because a model says so. Keep a paper/human route usable.
