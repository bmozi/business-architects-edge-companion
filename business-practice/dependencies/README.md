# Chapter 29: Expose cross-functional dependencies

This is a self-study kit for the book's reader-facing Chapter 29. All Harbor facts and counts are invented teaching material. No private workplace data, paid account or AI tool is required.

## Work the paper case first

R7 says receipt is not satisfaction. A status source returns documents_received=true; a proposed endpoint returns HTTP 202. Support supplies learner ID and a question, while Admissions needs requirement ID, status, rule version and a permitted return path. Staffing is unagreed and one help channel is inaccessible. Write three priority dependency rows and state which work may proceed before advertising the route.

1. Read the chapter, then spend about 30 minutes on your own answer.
2. Copy [worksheet.csv](worksheet.csv), fill the fields and retain your assumptions. Use a spreadsheet or plain text; blank template cells are for your work.
3. Challenge your first answer: Can the receiving owner actually accept this case type and volume? Does an HTTP response establish a business agreement or resolution? What happens to rejected or ambiguous work?
4. Revise before opening the [worked answer and rubric](worked-answer.md). Compare reasoning and evidence boundaries rather than copying a preferred conclusion.
5. Repeat with a safe fictional or sanitized case of your own. More than one conclusion can earn full credit when the reasoning is justified.

## Deliverable

Three challenged supplier/receiver records, clear meaning and capacity gates, a return/fallback path and a release decision that respects missing commitments.

AI may propose or challenge from permitted inputs. People validate meaning, owners, funding and commitments. An invented source, automatic approval or missing receiver does not become evidence because a model says so. Keep a paper/human route usable.

## Optional arithmetic and fixture check

From the repository root run `python3 scripts/check_resources.py`. This checks supplied synthetic counts and files. It does not score your worksheet or establish business value, safety, job readiness or learner effectiveness.
