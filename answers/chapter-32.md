# Chapter 32: Build a Credible Career Portfolio — worked answers

## Paper answer
Sample two-line portfolio entry: “Designed a simulated R7 onboarding architecture decision: mapped receipt-versus-satisfaction semantics to a negative API test, capability gap, owner gates and a smaller-first investment memo. Produced a documented teaching artifact and adverse-case analysis; no system was deployed and no learner outcome was measured.”

Thirty-second explanation: “I began with what the learner needed to understand, identified that ‘received’ could be mistaken for ‘satisfied,’ and compared a static repair/human handoff with a broader AI path. I documented which owner would need to accept meaning and routing and how I would measure harm. This was a simulation, so I use it to demonstrate reasoning, not to claim employer impact.” A stronger future work claim would require an authorized real decision/implementation record; a stronger outcome claim requires a defined baseline/follow-up and appropriate attribution, permission to disclose, and affected-person evidence.

## Transfer rubric (12 points)
Award 2 each for clear stakes; distinct personal contribution and team credit; genuine alternative; inspectable evidence anchor; explicit observed/proposed/simulated/unknown status; confidentiality and outcome/attribution limit. A qualitative prevention or mentoring story can score fully. Common wrong answers: claim Harbor is WGU; say “improved starts” without observation; call a sponsor conversation an enterprise approval; use a dollar number with no denominator/method. Feedback asks for the smallest truthful verb that the evidence supports.
