# Business architecture decision packets

These original fictional packets accompany the book's business architecture chapters. They are separate from the earlier two-year investment example and are not observed results, approved budgets or promises of profit.

- `decision-packets.json`: proposed capability/value-stage relationships, a finite quarterly portfolio and the maintenance-offering cost scenarios.
- Run `python3 scripts/check_business_architecture_examples.py` from the repository root to enumerate all portfolio subsets and recompute the contribution scenarios.
- Attempt the book's exercises before consulting its chapter answer guide. Feasibility calculations do not choose a strategy, create staff capacity or authorize a commitment.

Use the chapter templates to record definitions, owners, evidence and unknowns. A dash in the cross-map means a link was not asserted in that bounded view. Fixed overhead and demand probabilities are absent from the maintenance packet; the results are contributions before those omitted costs, not profit forecasts.
